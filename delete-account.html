RSMS — Delete your account

RSMS is a school results management system built by Rehoteq Technologies. Accounts belong to a school: a school administrator creates and manages staff, student and parent accounts inside the portal.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email rehoteq@gmail.com with the subject line "RSMS account deletion request".
  2. Include your name, your school ID (for example REH-2J8KQ2XF), and the account you want deleted.
  3. School administrators can also remove individual staff, student or parent accounts directly from the admin portal at any time.

What gets deleted or kept

When an account is deleted, the account's sign-in details, name, email address and phone number are removed. Results, fee and other school records are managed by your school's administrator; deletion of those records is handled together with the school. We may retain records we are legally required to keep. Your request is handled by the Rehoteq team.
